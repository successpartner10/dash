Smart Dash Cam

One app, two automatic modes. Driving (GPS · speed · loop recording · accident detection) and Parking (person / vehicle / impact detection · live remote video · two-way talk). And instead of 8 hours of video — an AI timeline that tells you what actually mattered.

Live view starts from your real camera. If no camera is available (or it's blocked), it falls back to the demo feed automatically.
